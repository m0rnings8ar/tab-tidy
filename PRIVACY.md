# Tab Tidy Privacy Policy

_Effective date: October 25, 2026_

Tab Tidy is a Chrome extension that finds duplicate tabs, groups tabs by site and saves sessions of tabs. It is built to work entirely on your device.

## The short version

- Tab Tidy does **not** collect, sell or share any of your data.
- Tab Tidy does **not** send anything over the internet. It has no servers, analytics, tracking or ads.
- Everything Tab Tidy stores stays in your browser on your device, and you can delete it at any time.

## What Tab Tidy accesses and why

| Data | Why it's needed | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Addresses (URLs) and titles of tabs in your current window | To find duplicate tabs, group tabs by site and save sessions | Read on your device when you open the popup or use a feature. Never sent anywhere. |
| Tabs you choose to save as a session (URL, title, pinned state), plus the session's name and when you saved it | So you can restore the session later | Stored on your device using Chrome's local extension storage |
| Your Tab Tidy settings (duplicate matching, confirmation and pinned-tab options) | To remember your preferences | Stored on your device using Chrome's local extension storage |

Tab Tidy reads tab information only when you open its popup or use one of its features. It does not keep a history of the pages you visit, and it never reads the content of web pages.

## Permissions

- **`tabs`**: lets Tab Tidy read tab addresses and titles, and close, open or move tabs when you ask it to.
- **`tabGroups`**: lets Tab Tidy create and name tab groups when you choose "Group by domain".
- **`storage`**: lets Tab Tidy save your sessions and settings on your device.

Tab Tidy does not request access to the content of websites, your browsing history, cookies, downloads or your Google account.

## Sharing and selling data

Tab Tidy does not sell, rent, transfer or share any user data with anyone. No data is used for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose other than the features described above.

The use of information received from Chrome APIs follows the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Links to other websites

The popup includes a "Buy me a coffee" link. Clicking it opens buymeacoffee.com in a new tab. Tab Tidy sends nothing to that site, and that site's own privacy policy applies to anything you do there.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your saved sessions and settings stay on your device until you remove them:

- Delete a saved session from the **Saved sessions** screen in the popup.
- Uninstall Tab Tidy to remove all of its stored data from your browser.

Because Tab Tidy has no servers, we hold no copy of your data and cannot recover deleted sessions.

## Children

Tab Tidy does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date. Any change that affects how data is handled will also be described in the extension's release notes.
